ICSO Auditions

ICSO is a fully auditioned orchestra. Audition slots are 9 minutes long. 5 minutes are for your own-choice piece (unaccompanied), and the rest is for some sight-reading and a small discussion with the audition panel. Sign up for an audition at the start of the academic year, see our audition FAQs below, and check out our Instagram for the latest updates.

How do I sign up for an audition?

Please read all the information on the form carefully. Auditions will be held on Wednesday 30th September, Thursday 1st October, Monday 5th October, and Wednesday 7th October. We recommend you sign up when you find out your timetable to avoid any clashes.
Auditions sign-up link: https://calendar.app.google/ncnSB4QiwPApeMsJ6

What grade should I be?

There is no required grade to join. The average standard of members is around Grade 8, and amongst us are several ex-members of the National Youth Orchestra, as well as graduates of the junior conservatoires.

But don't be put off - anyone is welcome to audition!

When are auditions held?

Auditions for 2026 will run in the first two weeks of the academic year.

Who will be on the audition panel?

Auditions are held jointly with Imperial College Sinfonietta, so the conductors and committee chairs for both IC Sinfonietta and ICSO will be present. Section leaders may also be present for some auditions.

Will I be able to warm up beforehand?

Yes. Arrive 10-15 minutes before your audition to warm up on your instrument. A practice room will be provided. To book more time in a practice room, contact the Blyth Centre as soon as possible.

Will there be an accompanist?

No. We ask everybody to audition without accompaniment to keep auditions fair for those who don't have an accompanist. Your piece does not have to originally be written as an unaccompanied work.

How long should my own-choice piece be?

Around 5 minutes long. The piece can be an excerpt from a larger work, if you wish. Just make sure it shows off what you can do!

When will I hear the outcome?

For 2026, in the second week of term. Entry into ICSO can be incredibly competitive, so don't worry if you don't get in the first time: you can try again later in the year if a spot opens, or at the start of the following year.

When and where does ICSO rehearse?

ICSO rehearses every Wednesday evening, from 18:30 to 21:00 in the Senior Common Room (South Kensington). We also have a small break in the middle, with some free baked treats by different members of the orchestra every week.

I'm not an Imperial student. Can I still audition?

Yes. We welcome external students to audition and get involved too.

I have another question...

Feel free to come and ask us any other questions that you might have at ICSO's stall at the Welcome Fair at the start of the academic year.

If there's anything else we can help with, please contact us.

We would also love to see you at ICSO's Open Rehearsal on the first Wednesday evening (with free pizza!) - auditions are NOT necessary for this first rehearsal. Click here to sign up for the open rehearsal!

Good luck, and looking forward to seeing you (at the Welcome Fair, our Open Rehearsal, or auditions) soon!

Concerto Performances

ICSO and IC Sinfonietta provide opportunities for those keen to be considered for a concerto performance, typically with one concerto soloist joining each orchestra per term. Concerto performances with other Imperial music ensembles may be arranged, but on a case-by-case basis.

Concerto auditions are held once a year in the Spring Term for performance in the following academic year. You will be asked to play for a total of 10-15 minutes across all movements of the concerto. Unlike the regular auditions to play in the orchestra, an accompanist will be provided for the concerto auditions (but you may bring your own if you wish).

Please get in touch if you'd like to learn more about concerto auditions.

Socials

"Come to Union!"

Every week, some generous ICSO members volunteer to provide many treats for the mid-rehearsal break, almost always including hot drinks and biscuits.

ICSO has two elected social secretaries dedicated to making sure you have a great time outside of rehearsals too. The ICSO social scene can range from formal dinners, post-rehearsal trips to Beit Quad (home of the Imperial College Union), or post-concert socials across London. Some ICSO socials also include joining forces with Imperial's other musical ensembles, like a 2026 trip to Thorpe Park with members of IC Wind Band and IC Musical Theatre Society.
